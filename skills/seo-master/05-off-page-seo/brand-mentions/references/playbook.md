# Playbook: Brand mentions and citations

## How to do it
1. Track unlinked mentions (alerts, search operators)
2. Request link where mention is positive and relevant
3. Strengthen presence on credible directories and communities
4. Keep brand facts consistent

## Common problems and fixes
- Negative mentions -> respond publicly and fix root issue
- Self-promotion in forums -> follow rules

## Output
Mention log with outreach status.

## Tools and data sources
Ahrefs, Semrush, Majestic, Google Search Console Links report, Google Alerts, outreach CRM or spreadsheet.

## Related
- Parent: `../SKILL.md` (scope, installed skills, method)
- `ROUTER.md` and `DEPENDENCIES.md` at the seo-master root for other categories that may apply.

## Verify
- Re-fetch or re-crawl the changed URLs and confirm the fix is live (status, rendered HTML, schema validator, GSC URL Inspection as relevant).
- Compare against the baseline you recorded before the change.
- Report what changed, the evidence, and any remaining issue in a short DONE summary.
